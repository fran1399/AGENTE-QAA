# Test Design

## Requirements per Case

- ID, objective, priority, risk, preconditions, data, steps, expected
- Test Data requirements declared for validation/traceability (Test Data Agent)
- Evidence required, confidence rationale

## Guidelines

- Design for verifiability (must be executable and checkable)
- Prioritize by risk
- Do not invent requirements
- Keep expected vs observed distinct
- NO EVIDENCE = NO CONFIRMED RESULT
- Avoid asserting execution/validation
- Trace to risks when applicable
